This project is a creative space to process my journey in ministry and theology; a deliberate step away from the pressure of perfectionism I feel in my class writings and into authenticity. I believe God made us all to create things that reflect both who we are and who He is. For me, that medium is writing. My hope is that these journals serve as a stained-glass window into my passions, honest wrestling, and story as it unfolds.

Hey there! I’m Daniel. I serve in high school ministry at Heartland Church, walking alongside students as they discover who Jesus is and who He’s shaping them to be.

At my core, I have a deep love for serving others, diving into God's Word, and exploring the richness of theology. As an aspiring missionary, I’m eager to see where God leads this passion for ministry—whether near or far.
