Publisher's Note

This project is a creative space to process my journey in ministry and theology; a deliberate step away from the pressure of perfectionism I feel in my class writings and into authenticity. I believe God made us all to create things that reflect both who we are and who He is. For me, that medium is writing. My hope is that these journals serve as a window into my passions and honest wrestling.
